# Sound effects

## Every visible action makes a sound
Doors, chests, levers, steps, hits, an arrow fired: if we see it, we hear it.

## Footsteps match the surface
Grass, stone, wood, sand sound different; pick the surface under the foot.

## Sync within one frame
The sound starts on the frame the action lands, never after.

## Fire and torches crackle
A burning torch near the camera has a quiet crackle loop.
