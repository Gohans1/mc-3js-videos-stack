# Mobs

## Mobs behave like in Minecraft

### Zombies walk slowly with arms out

### Skeletons strafe and shoot

### Spiders climb walls

## A creeper hisses before it explodes

It swells and flashes white for 1.5 s with the hiss, then blows up.
