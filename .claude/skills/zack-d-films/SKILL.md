## Top 10 Non-Negotiable Zack D. Films Vibe Laws

1. **Slow Down the Voice to Win (`N=43` MC-Friendly WPM):** Target **148–162 WPM** (`<150 WPM`: `100%` Winners, `29.2M` views; `150–164 WPM`: `42.9%` Winners, `20.5M`; `180+ WPM`: `0%` Winners, `57.1%` Flops, `5.2M`). Viewers need micro-breathing space to decode 3D visual reveals.
2. **Never Hard-Cut What a 3D Camera Can Bridge (`N=17` & `N=60`):** Keep **`64.6%–67.4%`** of shot transitions as `Continuous [NO CUT]` damped splines. Winners median only **`4.9` edit cuts/min** vs **`8.1` cuts/min** on Flops (`N=17`), while overall `N=17` median stays at **`23.5` camera beats/min** (`20.8` Winner median).
3. **Hold Shot 1 Long Enough to Read (`N=17`):** Winners hold the opening shot **`2.04s`** median (vs `1.37s` on Flops) before the first beat shift, opening directly in-action or on a concrete `"If you..."` / `"Imagine..."` visual trigger (`0/43` cliché `"Did you know..."` openers).
4. **Chain Every Beat with `But / Therefore` or `Causal Chain` (`N=17`):** `88.2%` of mined Shorts use `but_therefore` (`70.6%`) or `causal_chain` (`17.6%`, `4.46x` median outlier). Linear `"and then"` lists (`chronological_and_then`) scored **`0%` Winners**.
5. **Hit the Duration Sweet Spot or Go All-In (`N=270` MC-Friendly):** Avoid `<25s` (`61.5%` Flops, `0.45x`). Target **`35–44s`** (`44.1%` Winners, `1.25x`, `~95–110` words) for standard concepts, or commit to the **`55s+`** band (`55–73s` typical target; `n=15`: `86.7%` Winners, `0%` Flops, `3.05x`, `~145–175` words) when multi-stage escalation justifies it.
6. **Land the Twist or Reversal at ~74%–82% Runtime (`N=17`):** `88.2%` of Shorts contain a clear twist/reversal (median **`76%`** runtime), closing on an **`ironic_twist_punchline`** (`47.1%`, `1.71x` W/F lift) or **`satisfying_resolution`** (`41.2%`). Never force a gimmicky seamless loop (`0/17`).
7. **Smooth Damped Easing + Clean Studio Lighting (`N=17`):** **`94.1%`** overall (**`100%`** of Winners) use `smooth_damped` motion (`easing:snappy_overshoot` = `0%` Winners). **`clean_studio_soft`** lighting achieves **`2.29x`** W/F lift (`57.1%` Winners vs `25.0%` Flops).
8. **Guide the Eye with Emissive Pulses, Diegetic Labels, and 3D Graphics (`N=17`):** Winners outperform Flops on `has_emissive_highlight_pulse` (`100%` vs `75%`, `+25 pp`), `has_diegetic_text_or_labels` (`85.7%` vs `50%`, `+35.7 pp`), `has_motion_graphics_or_arrows` (`85.7%` vs `50%`, `+35.7 pp`), `has_time_remap_or_hit_stop` (`57.1%` vs `25%`, `+32.1 pp`), and `has_cutaway_or_xray` (`71.4%` vs `50%`, `+21.4 pp`).
9. **Use Classic Editorial Transitions, Never Flashy Whips (`N=17` & `N=60`):** Pair `Continuous [NO CUT]` moves with **`cross_dissolve`** (`71.4%` Winners vs `0%` Flops in `N=17`), **`card_flip`** (`35.3%` in `N=17`; `30.0%` in `N=60`), and **`wipe_up`/`push_up`** (`17.6%` unique in `N=17`, `23.5%` incl. `vertical_push`; `20.0%` `pushUpTransition` in `N=60`). Ban `flash_white` / `flash_whip` / `flash_bloom` (Flop-only in `N=17`).
10. **Package Tight: <=6–7 Words + 1 High-Arousal Trailing Emoji (`N=734`):** `98.4%` of MC-friendly Shorts end with 1 reaction emoji; `<=5` words (`1.20x`) and `6–7` words (`1.00x`) beat `8–9` word titles (`0.90x`). Favor `😱` (`1.29x`), `🤯` (`1.26x`), `🤨` (`1.18x`), or `😏` (`36.4%` W, polarized `0.93x` for tricks/hacks) over passive `😢` (`11.1%` W, `27.8%` F).

## 7-Step Production Workflow (Idea -> Ready-to-Build Blueprint)

Follow these 7 steps in strict order when a user gives you a topic or prompt:

1. **Classify Topic & Minecraft Feasibility:** Map the idea to a topic category (`hypothetical_what_if`, `explainer_how_it_works`, `history_weird_true_story`, `crime_disaster_accident`, `pop_culture_media_tech`, `animals_nature_survival`, `everyday_object_secret`, or `other`). Replace any microscopic anatomy/gear detail with macro architectural cutaways, props, HUD callouts, or character staging.
2. **Design Packaging & Hook (`0–3s`):** Consult [references/hooks-and-openings.md](references/hooks-and-openings.md) to pick the topic-matched hook archetype, write a <=6–7 word title + 1 trailing emoji, lock opening shot length to `1.7–2.5s`, and write a zero-cliché first spoken line paired with an immediate physical visual trigger.
3. **Architect Script & Beat Timeline:** Consult [references/storytelling-and-scripts.md](references/storytelling-and-scripts.md) to pick the winning structure (`problem_solution_irony`, `what_if_chain_reaction`, or `mechanism_step_by_step`), allocate exact beat `%` budgets (`hook 12%`, `setup 15%`, `escalation 27%`, `proof/value 14–21%`, `twist 12%` at `~76%`, `resolution 14%`), and lock total words to `148–162 WPM`.
4. **Choreograph Continuous 3D Camera & Boundaries:** Consult [references/cinematography-and-editing.md](references/cinematography-and-editing.md) to plan `>=65%` `Continuous [NO CUT]` splines, `<=5.0` ideal (`<=5.5` QA ceiling) edit cuts/min, `20–25` camera beats/min, framing mix (`M`/`MCU`/`W`/`CU`), `eye`/`high` dominant angles, `cross_dissolve`/`card_flip`/`push_up` transitions, shutter motion blur, and rack-focus DOF.
5. **Specify Lookdev, VFX & Minecraft 3D (`mc-films`) Mapping:** Consult [references/animation-lookdev-and-vfx.md](references/animation-lookdev-and-vfx.md) to specify `clean_studio_soft` or `natural_daylight` lighting, `smooth_damped` acting, emissive noun highlights (`film-stage/highlight`), architectural clip-plane cutaways (`localClippingEnabled`), diegetic labels/branding, and exact `mc-films` modules.
6. **Score Voice, BGM & SFX Sync:** Consult [references/audio-voice-and-pacing.md](references/audio-voice-and-pacing.md) to set ElevenLabs/GenVoice pacing (`148–162 WPM`), continuous or twist-shifting BGM bed, clean beat-aligned VO (no J/L-cut confusion, no dead pre-impact silence), and tactile foley accents verified with `earshot`.
7. **Run Pre-Production & Post-Render Vibe QA:** Audit the finished script, shot list, and render against every threshold in [references/anti-patterns-and-qa.md](references/anti-patterns-and-qa.md). Study the complete end-to-end worked proof at [references/examples/gta6-vs-gta5-blueprint.md](references/examples/gta6-vs-gta5-blueprint.md).
