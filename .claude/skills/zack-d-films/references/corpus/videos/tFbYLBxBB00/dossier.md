### 1. Hook, Packaging & Channel DNA Summary
- **Topic & Premise:** `hypothetical_what_if` — Tests what would actually kill you if you ate bananas non-stop: immediate stomach rupture (60 bananas) vs. the radiation-poisoning myth vs. the lethal radioactive threshold (35 million bananas at once). Emotional drivers are visceral physical curiosity, scientific wonder, and escalating absurdist comedy.
- **Hook Anatomy (`0–3s`):** `hypothetical_premise` — Opens at `00:00.042` with the verbatim line *"If you were forced to eat a banana, then another, and another..."* paired immediately with a `00:00.000–00:01.702 (f0–f51)` tight close-up where a mint-green robotic pincer holds a peeled banana in extreme foreground bokeh, rack-focuses to the protagonist's squinting face, tilts his chin back, and shoves it down his throat—planting an instant physiological curiosity gap (*how many would it take, and how would you die?*).
- **Storytelling & Retention Engine:** Built on `what_if_chain_reaction` causality chained strictly via `but_therefore` pivots across 5 timed re-hooks (`7.5s` VHS rewind, `16.0s` sci-fi wireframe shift, `25.2s` Chernobyl benchmark, `31.7s` lethal-threshold calculation, `39.6s` balloon-belly twist at `83%` runtime). Viewers stay to the final second (`ironic_twist_punchline`) for the subversive non-radiation death reveal, a diegetic Guinness World Records floor gag, and a background slapstick slip-and-flip payoff (`46.4s–47.8s`).
- **Visual, Camera & Motion Signature:** Predominantly `M` (`9/23`) and `CU` (`6/23`) shots with a slightly `wide` 3D lens feel and zero hard cuts (`0` Hard Cuts vs. `16` Continuous camera moves, `4` Hidden Cuts, and `2` visible Transitions). Features damped spline camera flights punctuated by kinetic whip-pans, soft cyan studio lighting alternating with mood-matched low-key organ/Chernobyl lighting, alpha-fade torso cutaways, emissive cyan/green outline pulses, and clean 3D renders free of optical grain outside the deliberate VHS rewind filter.
- **Soundscape, Voice & Secret-Sauce:** `shifting_bgm_plus_vo` — Crisp, deadpan-urgent male VO bridges across scene wipes/whip-pans (`has_jl_cut: true`) over a dynamic electronic underscore that shifts from tense synth curiosity to a VHS tape-rewind effect (`00:07.5`), sci-fi pulse (`00:16.0`), warning tension (`00:25.2`), and a comedic drop at the belly-inflation twist (`00:39.6`), backed by whooshes, pouring cascade SFX, and final crash impact. Reusable channel tricks: gamified multiplier badges (`1x` → `1,000,000x` → `35,000,000x`), embedded diegetic channel logos ("ZACK D FILMS" on shirt/banana), and a post-punchline background slapstick tag.
