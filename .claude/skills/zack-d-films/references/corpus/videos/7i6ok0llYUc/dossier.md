### 1. Hook, Packaging & Channel DNA Summary
- **Topic & Premise:** `other` (`social_experiment_moral_parable`) — A teacher challenges students to find their own named balloon in a crowded hallway within 5 minutes and fails almost everyone, then flips the rule to grabbing the nearest balloon and giving it to its owner, proving in 2 minutes that helping others brings your own happiness fastest. Emotional drivers are curiosity, relatable frustration, and warm moral satisfaction.
- **Hook Anatomy (`0–3s`):** `character_story_setup` — Opens voiced on *"She handed them balloons and told them to write their name on it"* (`opening_shot_sec`: `4.504` before the first transition), pushing smoothly from a wide classroom establish into an over-the-shoulder close-up as the protagonist writes `"Zack D."` on a balloon (`f0–f90`) highlighted by a sweeping green emissive glow band (`f30–f51`), planting curiosity around why this bizarre ritual is happening.
- **Storytelling & Retention Engine:** `problem_solution_irony` built on a tight `but_therefore` causal chain (*find your own -> BUT time expires with failure -> SO give away someone else's -> THEREFORE everyone gets theirs in 2 minutes*). Re-hooks reset visual energy every `4.5–7.2s` (`4.5s`, `10.7s`, `18.0s`, `26.8s`, `31.1s`), pivoting at the `49%` rule-flip twist (`00:18.018`) and resolving on a heartfelt two-shot exchange (`satisfying_resolution`) rather than a cheap loop.
- **Visual, Camera & Motion Signature:** Predominantly `M` (`6/11`) and `W` (`3/11`) `wide`-lens 3D scenes at `eye` (`6/11`) and `high` (`4/11`) angles using `smooth_damped` camera splines mixed with whip-pans (`7` `Continuous [NO CUT]` moves vs `3` `Transition` edits and `0` hard cuts). - **Soundscape, Voice & Secret-Sauce:** Calm, rhythmic male VO over `shifting_bgm_plus_vo` (curious plucked pulse during setup/search, thinning reflectively at the `00:00` failure, then swelling warmly into an uplifting chord resolution on the moral).
