### 1. Hook, Packaging & Channel DNA Summary
- **Topic & Premise:** `history_weird_true_story` (`phoebus_lightbulb_cartel`) — Explains how early light bulb manufacturers formed a secret international cartel to deliberately shorten bulb lifespans because durable bulbs hurt repeat sales, evoking outrage and fascination.
- **Hook Anatomy (`0–3s`):** `shock_fact_stat` — Opens at `f0` with *"They built a light bulb that lasted 2,000 hours,"* over a crisp `0.9s` workbench install (`f0–f26→f28`) that graphic-match-cuts directly into a room time-lapse where a split-flap counter races from `0000` to `2000 HOURS`, instantly planting the curiosity gap of why bulbs today don't last like that.
- **Storytelling & Retention Engine:** `problem_solution_irony` driven by tight `but_therefore` causality, with 5 sharp visual re-hooks (`3.9s`, `10.0s`, `20.2s`, `26.6s`, `33.0s`), a central expectation twist at `43%` (*"they agreed to make bulbs that burned out faster"*), and an `ironic_twist_punchline` finale where grinning cartel members are buried in raining cash behind a flickering, dying bulb.
- **Visual, Camera & Motion Signature:** Predominantly `M` and `MCU/CU` shots using wide-angle perspective and deep focus, driven by long `Continuous [NO CUT]` 3D spline camera journeys (`10` continuous camera bridges vs `6` stylized transitions and only `2` match-cut `Hard Cut`s), color-coded lighting shifts (warm domestic vs bright retail vs cool low-key conspiracy room), cyan X-ray cutaways, and rigid-body prop cascades.
- **Soundscape, Voice & Secret-Sauce:** Crisp, authoritative male VO with `J-cut` clause leads entering 2–6 frames ahead of transitions, backed by a `shifting_bgm_plus_vo` underscore (curious ticking energy shifting into darker conspiratorial synth tension) plus diegetic SFX; signature tricks include carrying exposition silently through foreground/background set dressing (`"PLANNED OBSOLESCENCE"` poster, legible `"1,000 hours"` contract) and layering X-ray failure directly over character facial reactions in one frame.
