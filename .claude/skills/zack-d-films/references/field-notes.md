## 2. Hard-Won Production Lessons, Tips & Tricks

| ID | Status | Lesson / Tip | Scope | Target Department | Practical Rule (What Goes Wrong & How to Prevent It) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `PL-001` | `Active` | Audit Continuity Before Adding Hard Cuts | `style-agnostic` | `cinematography-and-editing.md` | Hard cuts break the hypnotic spatial flow that defines the channel. Before inserting a cut, verify whether a continuous push/pull/orbit, pass-through, or peak-smear `Hidden Cut` bridges the two beats more cleanly while preserving a shared visual anchor. |
| `PL-002` | `Active` | Ramp Look, Hand & Prop Switches Over ~0.10s | `style-agnostic` | `animation-lookdev-and-vfx.md` | Single-frame gaze or grip snaps read like animation bugs. Transition eye-leads-head turns, hand switches, and prop handoffs over ~0.10s (~3 frames at 30 fps) with clear anticipation and settle. |
| `PL-003` | `Active` | Inspect Sub-0.5s Emissive Cues on 3 Consecutive States | `style-agnostic` | `anti-patterns-and-qa.md` | Short flashes (`0.3–0.8s`) often clip facial detail, spill onto unintended surfaces, or remain faintly lit into the next shot. Always review pre-ON, peak-ON, and post-OFF stills side-by-side before approving a shot. |
| `PL-004` | `Active` | Preserve Eye-Line, Headroom & 9:16 Safe Margins Across Proportions | `style-agnostic` | `cinematography-and-editing.md` | Copying raw camera distances across different character proportions (e.g., realistic human vs blocky Minecraft vs stylized 2D) ruins framing. Re-anchor framing by eye-line height, headroom ratio, lower-body crop point, and vertical Shorts UI chrome clearance. |
| `PL-005` | `Active` | Keep Narration Clinical While Visuals Carry the Spike | `style-agnostic` | `audio-voice-and-pacing.md` | Hyped or over-acted VO clashes with dense visual reveals and accelerates listener fatigue. Keep vocal delivery calm, articulate, and matter-of-fact; let prop physics, facial reactions, and crisp foley/whooshes deliver the impact. |
| `PL-006` | `Active` | Measure Real Spoken WPM From Aligned Audio, Not Draft Word Count | `style-agnostic` | `audio-voice-and-pacing.md` | Script word counts mislead once dramatic pauses and breath gaps enter the read. Measure final spoken WPM from timed audio alignment (excluding end holds) and trim dense clauses whenever pacing creeps toward the >=180 WPM Flop cliff. |
| `PL-007` | `Active` | Bind Point-Target DOF, Multi-Sample Shutter Blur & Correct Prop-vs-Character Highlights | `adapter:mc-films` | `animation-lookdev-and-vfx.md (§7.1)` | In `mc-films`, bind `optics.focus` / `film-stage/dof` to named 3D targets or explicit world points rather than static guesses, set `shutter: { angle: 180, samples: 8..64, apertureSamples: 8..16 }` (passing `--samples 8` on both `render stills` and `render clip --span` for genuine motion blur), use `film-stage/highlight` (`createHighlight` + `pulseEnvelope`) ONLY for whole-surface prop/block emissive pulses (**never pass `steve.g`** — cloning `mesh.material` detaches `player.ts` `inner`/`outer` so `showParts`/`red`/`setSkin` break and faces blow out!), and build `FN-001` outlines (`EdgesGeometry`/inverted-hull `THREE.BackSide`) and `FN-002` character rim snaps (dedicated cyan rim light or face-excluding `BackSide` shell) cleanly without mutating shared player materials. |
| `PL-008` | `Active` | Protect Semantic Highlight Hues From Scene Lighting & Bloom | `style-agnostic` | `animation-lookdev-and-vfx.md` | Never let scene neon, signage, or background lighting share your semantic `FN-005` highlight hue (e.g., dress background shopfronts in warm coral/magenta when yellow is reserved for `FN-001` outlines and cyan for `FN-002` snap flashes), and keep non-blooming emphasis highlights safely below the scene post-processing bloom threshold unless intentional glow is desired. |
| `PL-009` | `Active` | Extend Pre-Cut Moving Keyframes to `cut - 0.001s` & Use Output Camera Clock on Retimed Shots | `adapter:mc-films` | `animation-lookdev-and-vfx.md (§7.1)` | In `mc-films`, extend moving camera and object keyframes right up to `cut - 0.001s` before `cut: true` (`skill-data/core/references/render.md:40`) so the final pre-cut frame keeps genuine directional shutter motion blur instead of popping razor-sharp, and set per-shot `cameraClock: "output"` (`film.md:63`) on `timing.json` Speed/Freeze ramps so camera flight stays smooth across retimes. |
| `PL-010` | `Active` | Make Handheld Props Contrast With Block Skin & Clothes, Offset Along the Fingers | `adapter:mc-films` | `animation-lookdev-and-vfx.md` | A Minecraft hand is ~2x a human one and its skin tone swallows amber drinks, and a white pad vanishes on a white shirt or sinks inside the torso via `holdProp`. Pick a prop value/hue far from the holder's skin and outfit (dark cola, birch clipboard), and push the `holdProp` offset along the fingers so crossed-arm poses never hide it; confirm on native stills at the gesture's peak. |
| `PL-011` | `Active` | Re-Solve Ref Camera Angles for Block Heads Instead of Copying Them | `adapter:mc-films` | `cinematography-and-editing.md` | A 0.47 m block head and chunky arms occlude over-shoulder angles that work on a human, a pure side profile shows no eyes, and block props run ~1.8x real size. Move cameras higher, farther, or ~15-20 deg toward the front (3/4 profile) so the face reads, and re-check that occluders placed for a whip (e.g., a dark post that blacks the frame for a cut) are crossed only at the whip, not earlier in the move. |
