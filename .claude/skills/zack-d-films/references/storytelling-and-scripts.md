## 6. Twist Placement & Ending Mechanics (`N=17` Deep DNA)

- **Twist Presence:** `88.2%` (`15/17`), landing at median **`76.0%`** runtime (Winners: `76.0%`, Flops: `71.5%`).
- **Ending Type Distribution:**
  - **`ironic_twist_punchline`** (`8/17`, `47.1%`; `42.9%` Winners vs `25.0%` Flops -> **`1.71x` W/F lift**): Final beat flips the premise on its head or reveals a darkly funny catch.
  - **`satisfying_resolution`** (`7/17`, `41.2%`; `1.39x` median outlier): Cleanly solves the visual mystery or closes the moral/evolutionary "WHY".
  - **`question_cta`** (`2/17`, `11.8%`, directional): Works ONLY as a high-stakes survival dilemma (`SGgYDfeHYXA` `"What do you do?"` on protagonist's face, `7.76x`), fails as a click-below promo (`LjHDUQ7Htvk`, `0.35x`).
  - **`seamless_loop`:** **`0/17` (`0.0%`)** — do not bend natural ending grammar just to loop back to word 1.
