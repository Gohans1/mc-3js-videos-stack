## 6. Title Packaging & Trailing Emoji Laws (`N=734` MC-Friendly Mature Catalog)

1. **Length Discipline (`N=734`):** Winners median **`6` words** (`25.7M` median views) vs `7` words for Medians/Flops.
   - `<=5` words (`n=140`): **`24.3%` Winners, `1.20x`**, `11.4M` views.
   - `6–7` words (`n=442`): **`19.5%` Winners, `1.00x`**, `10.0M` views (channel backbone).
   - `8–9` words (`n=147`): **`16.3%` Winners, `0.90x`**, `9.4M` views (wordy drag).
   - `10+` words (`n=5`, directional): `40.0%` Winners (`0%` Flops), `1.40x`, `12.0M` views (rare long-form titles).
2. **Title Openers (`N=734`, all 8 buckets):**
   - **`Can/Could/Would ...`** (`n=28`): **`42.9%` Winners, `1.40x`**, `14.0M` views.
   - **`What ...`** (`n=32`): **`31.3%` Winners, `1.39x`**, `15.0M` views.
   - **`The ...`** (`n=145`): `20.0%` Winners, `1.10x`, `11.0M` views.
   - **`He/She/They/This ...`** (`n=70`): `17.1%` Winners, `1.10x`, `10.0M` views.
   - **`Why ...`** (`n=84`): `13.1%` Winners, `1.00x`, `10.5M` views.
   - **`How ...`** (`n=146`): `21.2%` Winners / `21.9%` Flops, `0.91x`, `9.6M` views (largest workhorse bucket).
   - **`Other`** (`n=135`): `17.8%` Winners / `22.2%` Flops, `0.92x`, `9.3M` views.
   - **`How To ...`** (`n=94`): `18.1%` Winners / `23.4%` Flops, **`0.87x`**, `9.1M` views (underperforms unless paired with clever social sabotage like *Hide And Seek*).
3. **1 Trailing Reaction Emoji Packaging Norm (`98.4%` of `N=734`, `98.6%` of Winners):**
   - Keep 1 trailing reaction emoji to match the channel's `98.4%` packaging signature (the rare `NONE` subgroup, `n=12` / `1.6%`, still posted `1.31x` median with `16.7%` W / `8.3%` F, so omitting an emoji alone did not cause `LjHDUQ7Htvk`'s `0.35x` flop — its 16 hard cuts and 3s dead promo CTA did).
   - **High-Lift Emojis:** `😱` (`n=41`, `26.8%` W / `9.8%` F, **`1.29x`**), `🤯` (`n=28`, `28.6%` W / `14.3%` F, **`1.26x`**), `🤨` (`n=48`, `22.9%` W, **`1.18x`**), `😏` (`n=22`, **`36.4%` W / `22.7%` F, `0.93x`** — polarized, high-ceiling for tricks/hacks).
   - **Baseline Workhorses:** `😮` (`26.6%` share, `0.96x`), `🤔` (`22.5%` share, `0.89x`), `😨` (`9.7%` share, `0.92x`).
   - **Avoid Unless Strongly Emotional:** `😢` (`n=36`, only **`11.1%` W vs `27.8%` F**, `0.92x`).
