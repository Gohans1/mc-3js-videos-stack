# Hooks, Packaging & Openings (Zone 1: Use)

Empirical title packaging, retention psychology, unbiased era stability, Dual-Path Title Confirmation, opening-shot framing, and hook archetype playbook for Zack D. Films (`statsVersion: 4`). Every metric states its exact denominator (`N=855` mature catalog, `N=316` mature enriched, `N=50` clean mature transcripts excluding 1 corrupt caption, `N=61` voiced mined dossiers, or `N=62` pure `schema_version: 2` deep DNA), subsample baseline, Fisher exact `p`, and Benjamini-Hochberg FDR `q`. Reliability tiers: `stable` (`n >= 8`), `tentative` (`n = 5–7`), `directional` (`n < 5`). Treat Winner-vs-Flop spreads as observational prioritization signals, not causal proof.

## 1. Two-Gate Shorts Retention, 3-Check Frame-0 Gate & Premise TAM

Shorts swipe feeds do not give viewers a browse thumbnail click decision — the **opening picture + title overlay IS the click decision**:
- **Gate 1 — Stayed-to-Watch (`0.0–1.3s`, 3-Check Frame-0 Scroll-Stop Gate):** Opening framing (`opening_shot_sec` median `1.73s`, `1.97s W`) must pass all 3 checks BEFORE writing shot 2:
  1. **`0.0–0.5s` (`f0–f15`) Visible Anomaly or Tension:** Frame 0 opens mid-action inside a bounded world with the central anomaly, hazard, or character crisis ALREADY visible on screen — never deferred to `2.0s+` after a generic establishing walk.
  2. **Mute Test:** Scrubbing `0.0–2.0s` on mute must unmistakably telegraph the subject, physical stakes, and what looks wrong.
  3. **Squint / Focal-Budget Check (`<=3` Elements):** At mobile thumbnail scale, at most `<=3` clear visual anchors compete for attention inside the 9:16 Safe Box (zero cluttered collage or tiny unreadable props).
- **Gate 2 — The Second Decision (`10–20%` runtime, ~`3.5–8.0s`):** Once the hook lands, viewers decide whether to stay through the middle. Lock a **causal escalation** immediately after `hook` — inside `cold_open_crisis` (`N=62`), `6/6` Winners (`100%`) keep `setup <= 15%` runtime vs `0/3` Flops (`p=0.012, q=0.643`), and inside `visual_pattern_interrupt`, `5/6` Winners (`83.3%`) hit `escalation` before `explanation_value` vs `0/3` Flops (`p=0.048, q=0.857`).
- **The Three Opening Promises:** Within `0–4s`, establish (1) **Subject** (who/what is on screen), (2) **Anomaly or Hazard** (what looks wrong or at risk), and (3) **Open Loop** (the implied question whose payoff lands late — Winner median twist at `73%`).
- **Broad-Audience Like-Rate Dilution (`N=316`):** Catalog Winners hold a **lower** median like rate (`2.66%`, `n=120`) than Medians (`3.42%`, `n=91`) and Flops (`3.75%`, `n=105`). Do NOT chase higher like rate by narrowing topics to niche diehards — viral reach pushes Shorts beyond core subscribers into broad cold audiences, naturally diluting like % while multiplying total views (`26.6M` vs `4.2M`).

### 1.1 ZDF Premise Stress-Test & Reframe Engine (Run at Mode A Gate 1)

Before pitching angles, test the user's raw premise against **4 Gate-1 Questions**: (1) **Everyday TAM Anchor** — does a cold viewer immediately recognize the object, body part, vehicle, game, or everyday situation? (2) **Visible Physical Engine** — can we *see* the mechanism, trap, or failure unfold on screen (cutaway, physical chain reaction, human body, or side-by-side comparison) rather than abstract facts? (3) **Escalating Stakes or Paradox** — does Step 1 physically force Step 2 (`But / So`), or is it a flat trivia list? (4) **Withheld Payoff** — does the title open a curiosity gap without giving away the twist?
