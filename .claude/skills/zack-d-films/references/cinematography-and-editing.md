# Cinematography, Camera Splines, and Editing Grammar

Every metric cites its exact source sample (`N=17` stratified deep DNA = `294` non-opening boundaries / `311` shots; `N=60` supplementary visual craft benchmark = `801` non-opening boundaries / `696` GT panels verified at `98.9%–99.4%`).

## 1. The 5-State Boundary Signature (Dual-Sample Cross-Validation)

Zack D. Films is **not** a jump-cut Shorts channel. Two completely independent samples converge within ~2–3 percentage points: **roughly two-thirds of every shot change is an unbroken 3D camera journey (`Continuous [NO CUT]`)**.

| Boundary Type | `N=17` Deep DNA (`294` non-opening) | `N=60` Visual Benchmark (`801` non-opening) | Production Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Continuous [NO CUT]` | **`64.6%`** (`190`) | **`67.4%`** (`540`) | **`>=65%`** (`65%–80%` typical) — bridge beats via damped dollies, whip-orbits, crane booms, and wall cutaways |
| `Transition` (editorial FX) | **`20.4%`** (`60`) | **`21.5%`** (`172`) | **`10%–25%`** — clean graphic-matched `cross_dissolve`, `card_flip`, or `push_up`/`wipe_up` |
| `Hidden Cut` (whip/occlusion) | **`6.5%`** (`19`) | **`8.2%`** (`66`) | **`5%–10%`** — mask location/time swaps inside fast whip-blur peaks or foreground pass-bys (wall, torso, door) |
| `Hard Cut` (direct splice) | **`8.5%`** (`25`) | **`2.9%`** (`23`) | **`<=5%`** (`0–2` per Short max; note `22/25` hard cuts in `N=17` came from just 2 Flops: `LjHDUQ7Htvk` `16` + `QXEr924US38` `6`!) |

- **Critical Winner vs Flop Proof (`N=17`):**
  - **All `7` Winners combined** used only **`1` Hard Cut total** (`UgP7_6_i1J4` had `1`; `6/7` Winners had **`0` Hard Cuts**!).
  - **Median Edit Cuts/Min (`hard_cut + hidden_cut + transition`):** Winners **`4.9` cuts/min** vs Flops **`8.1` cuts/min** (`7.5` overall median).
  - **Overall Median Camera Beats/Min (`edit_cuts + continuous`):** **`23.5` camera beats/min** overall (`20.8` Winner median; `27.1` Flop median; ~`1` motivated camera reframe or spatial beat every **`2.55 seconds`** overall!).
  - **Law:** Keep visual energy high through **continuous camera reframing (`~20–25` beats/min)** while keeping actual editorial cuts **low (`<=5.0` ideal, `<=5.5` QA ceiling)**.

## 5. Signature Continuous Camera Moves Observed Across Winners (`N=17`)

1. **Drywall / Roof Slice Penetration (`0BZ1C83I2R4`, `OXRzmyOMKVw`):** Camera pushes straight through an exterior wall or dollhouse roof using a clipping plane (`renderer.localClippingEnabled = true`) to reveal interior action without cutting.
2. **Off-Frame Prop Aging / Set Swap (`rPWvvcttpmk`, `320fjAdEboQ`):** Camera pans down onto a table/floor for a short time-lapse or whip-obscuration while swapping props or aging the character off-frame, then booms back up to reveal the changed state.
3. **160°–180° Smeared Whip-Orbit (`epP7TB8n3ZU`, `SGgYDfeHYXA`):** Fast damped whip-pan with multi-sample shutter blur bridges two sides of a room or pivots 180° onto a reacting face for the twist.
4. **Screen-Locked Metric Ascent (`UgP7_6_i1J4`, `320fjAdEboQ`):** Camera booms vertically alongside a floating 3D ruler or window-height counter (`6 FT -> 4 FT`, `1 FT -> 6 FT`) so spatial proof reads instantaneously.
