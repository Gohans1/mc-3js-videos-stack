# film-kit

Reusable modules for draw(t) films (live-studio); each file's header gives the method, its source and the exact API.

| File | When to use | How |
|---|---|---|
| src/curve.ts | Any keyed value (pose angle, prop, light) that must not stop dead at every key. | `curve([{t, v, stop?, hold?, linear?}], {angle?})(t)`, `.vel(t)` |
| src/spring.ts | A value that springs to targets or gets kicked; something that lags a moving target. | `springTrack([{t, to?, kick?}], {smooth, ratio})(t)`; `follow(target, {from, to})(t)` |
| src/transition.ts | Switching between two motions without a pop or a dead stop. | `chain([{t, f}], dur)(t)` |
| src/shake.ts | Camera jolts from impacts (a slam, a fall). | `shake([{t, amount}])(t)` gives [yaw, pitch, roll] in degrees |
| src/handheld.ts | A camera that feels held by a person (CC0 recorded wobble). | `handheld("THE_CLOSEUP", {amount, offset})(t)` gives {pos, rot, quat} in camera axes |
| src/lens.ts | Choosing a lens in mm, a dolly zoom, or real depth-of-field blur. | `camera.fov = fovOf(50, aspect)`; `dollyZoom(d0, fov0, fov1)`; `blurPx({...})` |
| src/safe.ts | Placing captions or titles. | `safe(W, H)` gives the EBU R 95 box; `inSafe(box, W, H)` |
| src/pose.ts | Blending a character's pose keys so a joint keeps moving through a key on its way instead of stopping at each. | `throughPose(prev, a, b, next, [tp, ta, tb, tn], t)`; `addPose(a, b)`; `scalePose`, `mask(spans)`, `canonical(p)`; scalars: `through(...)` in curve.ts |
| src/overlap.ts | Any character body pose: the chest, head and arms trail and settle on their own, no keys. | `const lag = overlap(poseAt, end)`; `addPose(poseAt(t), lag(t))` before hand and head IK |
| src/idle.ts | Any character body pose: breathing (faster and deeper after effort), weight shifts standing still, a dip when a fast move stops; a fright's gasp, held breath and relief exhale. | `const life = idle(poseAt, end, { standing, gasps: [{t, k}] })`; `addPose(poseAt(t), life(t))`; `life.gasp(t)`, `life.lungs(t)`; `freeze` (freeze.ts) takes the sway out; needs planted-foot leg IK |
| src/cartoon.ts | Optional, low: anticipation and follow-through on every move (Cartoon Animation Filter); reads cartoony if strong. | `const toon = cartoon(poseAt, end, { amount: 0.25 })`; `addPose(poseAt(t), toon(t))` |
| src/gaze.ts | A character's eyes: fixations, saccades that lead each head turn, eyes held on the target while the head turns, a blink with big shifts, lids that follow the eyes up and down; gliding after a moving thing (pursuit), looking away while thinking. | `gaze(shifts, end, { blinkedNear, pursuit: [[t0, t1]], think, freeze })(t, aim)` gives `{ look, blink, lid, pursuing, thinking }`; `aim(f, off, mode)` answers "pursue" with the target where it is at f and adds "avert" offsets in full; `lidOpen(lid)` adds to the face's open; `anglesOf`, `eyeTime`; worked example in demo/main.ts |
| src/startle.ts | A bang, a slam, a shock: the reflex jolt down the body in its fixed order (lids, head, arms, trunk, knees), weaker on repeats except the blink. | `const jolt = startle([{t, k}])`; `addPose(pose, jolt(t).pose)` before IK, `blink = max(blink, jolt(t).blink)`; key the turn to the source at `orientAt(e)`; pass the same events to idle's `gasps` |
| src/steer.ts | A walker with no look keyed: the head turns into a bend before the body, the eyes on the path 1.5 s ahead. | `const ahead = steer((t) => ({ x, z, speed }))`; head look at `ahead(t).head`, eyes at `ahead(t).at`, weight `ahead(t).w`; a keyed look wins |
| src/balance.ts | A standing character who reaches or bends: the hips go the other way from the mass so he stays over his feet. | `const bal = balance((t) => [left, forward] centre-of-mass offset from rest, end, { standing })`; `addPose(pose, bal(t))` before leg IK; weigh the segments by `WINTER` |
| src/blinks.ts | Any character's blinks: the rate follows what he does (rest, eyes busy, talking), blinks come at the breaks after an action, uneven gaps, a fast close and slow open. | `const lids = blinks(end, { rate: (t) => BLINKS.rate.focus or .rest, breaks: [t] })`; `blink = max(blink, lids(t))`; `gaze(..., { blinkedNear: lids.near })` |
| src/handeye.ts | A character who takes, puts or presses something: the eyes land on it ~0.5 s before the hand moves and leave just before it arrives. | `const R = reaches((t) => hand in his own axes, end, { rest, spans })`; `handEye(R in world)` gives look keys `{t, at}` for where nothing else is keyed; over a mocap clip they go to the eyes, the head only past the eyes' range |
| src/freeze.ts | A threat he can't act on yet (a noise, something in the dark): he goes still, sway and drift drop, the eyes hold, few blinks. | `const still = freeze([{t, until, k}])`; pass `freeze: still` to idle and gaze; scale micro's drift by `1 - FREEZE.drift * still(t)`; blink at the focus rate while frozen |
| src/notice.ts | Something appears or moves at the edge of his view: eyes then head go to it on their own, a short look, then back; weaker each time it repeats nearby. | `const glance = notice([{t, at, k, kind}])`; blend `glance(t)` ({at, w}) over the look; add `glance.shifts` to gaze's shifts |
| src/attention.ts | A character who looks at something: the chest and hips join a big head turn, later and less (eyes, head, chest, hips). | `const chain = attention((t) => ({ down, yaw, w }), end)`; `addPose(pose, chain(t))` before head IK; aim from headTo |
| src/steady.ts | Any character body pose: the head holds level against fast trunk tilts (pitch and roll), not against a look. | `const s = steady(poseAt, end)`; add `s(t).head` after head IK, its pitch times (1 - look weight) |
| src/lean.ts | A character who walks or runs: the body tilts into speeding up, braking and turns. | `const l = lean((t) => ({ x, z, yaw }), end)`; `addPose(pose, l(t))` |
| src/micro.ts | Any character held still: a tiny coherent drift (Perlin) so a hold never freezes. | `const drift = micro({ seed })`; `addPose(pose, drift(t))` last |
| src/arc.ts | Hand (or any IK) targets between two keys: the path swings round the shoulder instead of a straight slide. | `arc(shoulder, keyA, keyB, k)` with the blend's own eased k; the shoulder as posed at that frame |
| src/effort.ts | One mood knob (Laban Effort: space, weight, time, flow, plus sink) that sets every layer's options. | `const fx = effort(MOOD.tense)`; pass `fx.overlap`, `fx.cartoon`, `fx.idle`, `fx.gaze`, `fx.micro`; `phrase(keys, fx.hold)`; `addPose(pose, fx.posture)` |
| demo/ | Watching every character layer on scenes from a film, before and after side by side, each layer switchable. | `bun run demo`, open localhost:4350 (`?scene=dig|idle|walk|reach|scare|mocap`, `?mood=tense|tired`, `?t=`, `?view=face`, `?off=gaze,cartoon`) |

- Link into a film like live-studio: `bun link` here once, `"film-kit": "link:film-kit"` in the film; import `film-kit/<name>`.
- Every module stays a pure function of film time (or bakes once); no three.js import outside tests.
- `bun test` must pass after any change; a new rule gets a test that fails without it.
- data/shakify.json is Camera Shakify's CC0 data (licence beside it); their GPL code is not used.
- Character layers stack on the body pose: base, + overlap, + cartoon, + idle, + lean, + mood posture, + startle, + balance (measured on that sum), + attention (each baked from the same base pose function); then leg, head and hand IK with steady and micro added after the head; poses with breathing written in should drop it when idle's breath is on.
- Mocap clips (Mixamo, UAL) already carry overlap, breath, sway and drift: fade those layers, and idle's gasps and balance, out over each clip span with `scalePose(layer(t), mask(spans)(t))`; gaze (pursuit and thinking too), lids, blinks, notice, attention and steer stay on. Freeze can't take a clip's own sway out: over a clip only the eyes freeze. Handeye over a clip needs `spans` (the clips where he takes or touches something: a startle's flung arms also end out in front) and goes to the eyes, the head turning only by what the eyes can't cover. Startle stays on over clips (a clip cannot know about the bang) unless the clip is itself a startle. Blend into and out of clips by quaternion slerp (the film's slerpPose), never Euler lerp.
- A baked layer reads its input through `canonical()` (pose.ts): slerped poses come out with the same rotation spelled two ways (x, y, z) and (180 - x, y + 180, z + 180), and a spring chasing that jump whips the joint.
- demo/film/ is a copy of one film's rig code and keys, never a link: films are not touched from here. demo/public/vendor (Mojang's Steve skin, MCprep's rig) and demo/public/motion (three Mixamo clips: free in a film, never redistributed as files) are fetched by demo/setup.ts and gitignored; never commit them.
