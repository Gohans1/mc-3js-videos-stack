project films it one section at a time. Built so far: sections 1.1 and 1.2, 0:00-1:47.5 (CCTV CELL 0412, he wakes, tries the

# Techniques and Architecture

- Pure time function draw(t): every pose, face, light, lens effect, camera and caption is a closed-form function of film time t and src/edit.json, allowing instant seeking, single-frame stills, and isolated chunk re-renders.
- Shots (src/scene.ts SHOTS; the studio reads each shot's name from its line comment): 0 CCTV from the ceiling corner (lens bend, grain, caption), 1 clean close-up on Steve's eyes, 2 Steve's own view. In shot 2 his head and upper arms are scaled to nothing so the lens never sees inside them.
- Camera (src/camera.ts `cameraAt`): edit.json `cam` keys {t, p, l, fov, wave, roll?, cut?}; one centripetal Catmull-Rom per cut, a slow breathing sway scaled by `wave`. `p` and `l` may be {eye: [dx, dy, dz]}: offsets from Steve's eye point, measured once from the rig lying down (head bone, half a head up, `steve.eyeFromNeck` towards the crown), so the close-up and his own view follow the rig instead of hand-typed numbers.
- The cell (src/world.ts): one block is one metre; inside x 0..8, z 0..11, y 0..5. Bed in the middle, head north; desk, screen and chair on the west wall; steel door in the south wall; CCTV in the north-east ceiling corner (no cobweb there, it sits in front of the lens). Trap: block-model-renderer gives a merged scene that holds a glowing block one shared emissive material and the whole room glows in banded steps, so the sea-lantern lamp is a separate createScene and the ceiling leaves its cells empty.
- The film's own props (pack/assets/gohans + src/pack.ts): vanilla Java block models (blockstate with facing variants + model, elements on a 16 px grid) that only reference the game's textures by name, so the pack holds no Mojang files, Blockbench opens them, and block-model-renderer lights them like every other block. New prop: write its blockstate and model JSON (or cherry-pick JSON/PNG from E:\mc_vault\packs\ instead of bundling full mod jars), import both in src/pack.ts, place it in world.ts. For props with animated parts (e.g. the desk drawer pried open in section 1.2), load separately via loadModel(scene, assets, data, { mergeElements: false }) so draw(t) can translate the drawer mesh independently. Judge it up close with `ENTRY=lab/prop.ts AT=0 node src/render.mjs stills` (renders/prop_N.jpg).
- Moving props (edit.json `props`, documented at the top of src/props.ts): props.ts builds them and writes each item's story (chair, drawer, button, scrap, spilt pages, folders); src/screen.ts is the CRT's terminal and glow, src/ink.ts prints the pages, note and folder labels, both in the game's font (src/gamefont.ts); src/carry.ts moves a page or folder lying, in a hand (hang/flat/face), put down or thrown, from his hand grips alone so nothing teleports. Shared easing: src/ease.ts. src/edit.json stays one file (the studio's contract reads only that file).
- Steve (edit.json `steve`): {at, yaw, eyeFromNeck, twitch (right hand twitches), eyesOpen (lids snap open over 0.09 s), pupil {dark, lit, narrow} (pupils narrow under the lamp after the eyes open)}, plus the performance keys src/steve.ts documents (moves, hands, look, face, read).
- Feet (src/feet.ts + player.ts `legTo`): foot locking and two-bone leg IK, the way games stop foot sliding (D. Holden, theorangeduck.com "Inverse Kinematics and Foot Locking"). A sole on the floor stays where it landed while the body moves over it; in a walk the gait phase (player.ts `gaitOf`, STRIDE 0.5 m) says which foot stands; a foot steps in an arc to its next footfall, found by looking ahead, one foot at a time. draw(t) keeps no state, so the whole film is baked once per edit (60 Hz, ~0.6 s). Trap: never answer a sliding or robotic move by typing more keys; a move key that shifts `at` without `walk` is a weight shift of a few cm (feet.ts steps if it drifts 16 cm), anything longer is a walk. Trap: a standing leg is already straight (at full reach), so the "leg pulled too far" test needs 2 cm slack or he shuffles forever.
- Voice (voice/): SCRIPT.md lines are edit.json `takes` [{id, t, text}] with the script's English audio tags. voice/gen.py makes them (`--try` writes trials to voice/try/), voice/words.py times their words, voice/assemble.py places each take so its first word lands on `t` and writes assets/public/agent/full_voice.wav and `voice.lines`; voice_map.py feeds the studio's word map.
- Sound (sound/mix.py, edit.json `audio`): {voiceGain, lufs, beds [{name, from, to, gain, fadeIn, fadeOut, filters?}], sfx [{at, name, gain, start?, length?, fadeOut?}]} in one ffmpeg graph. The narration gets dialogue compression and its own limiter. The loudness pass must stay linear: ffmpeg-normalize's dynamic fallback lifts every quiet stretch (it brought the CCTV hiss up level with the voice); a mostly quiet excerpt therefore takes a lower `lufs` (section 1.1 alone: -16). The room hum is low-passed (the script's "deep, muffled" hum, and its boosted noise floor would hide the CCTV hiss). The studio's sound.py runs `sound/mix.py --raw`; the studio remixes when sound.py or an audio file changes, not on edit.json `audio` edits (re-save sound.py).
- Draft render and self-review: `AT=1,3.6 node src/render.mjs stills` (renders/still_*.jpg, review/sheet.py contact sheets), `node src/render.mjs clip` (30 s GPU chunks joined by stream copy, CHUNKS= re-renders only some), `python sound/mix.py renders/draft_video.mp4` (renders/draft.mp4), `python review/jumps.py renders/draft.mp4` (pops between frames; the eyes snapping open at 0:03.767 is planned). lab/diag.ts prints bone positions for placing Steve and the camera by numbers. Motion is judged by numbers and dense frames, never by a few stills (stills cannot show sliding): `SLIDE=0,108 AT=1 node src/render.mjs stills` prints every sole that slides over the floor, sinks into it or cannot be reached (scene.ts `feetCheck`; it should print nothing); `TRACE=from,to,step` prints his soles and their planted targets; `INSPECT=px,py,pz,lx,ly,lz,fov` shoots the stills from one fixed review camera instead of the film's.
- Studio: `node src/live.mjs 4324` in a Terminal, reached by the dev only over Tailscale (`tailscale serve --bg --https=8446 http://127.0.0.1:4324`); 443, 8443-8445, 8447 and 8448 (mc-asset-browser) belong to other projects.
- Zero-bloat vault links (assets/public/): Serves mc (client.jar), mcasset (1.21.11), and rig (MCprep player.glb) via gitignored NTFS junctions from E:\mc_vault; tools links to the headless Chrome on E:; E:\mc_vault\packs holds the 42 Modrinth packs that passed triage (579 MB: furniture, doors, lab, hospital, security, weapons, sounds) for prop cherry-picking without project bloat; rejected packs and why are in E:\mc_vault\packs_triage.md (never re-download them). Browse every block, item and sound with C:\Users\ADMIN\Desktop\mc-asset-browser (`bun server.ts`, port 4340, Tailscale https 8448); favorites land in E:\mc_vault\favorites.json.
