# Film Capability Atlas

Read this once when starting a new film. Every tool, workspace package export, procedural acting rig, shader hook, asset library, and repo skill available inside `films/<name>/` is indexed below so you never miss a hidden capability or reinvent an existing module. Keep `draw(t)` pure and seek-independent; treat Studio and built-in timelines as a starting pad, never a ceiling.

## 1. Visual, Optical, Lighting, and 3D Rendering (`film-stage`, `live-studio` + Bundled Engines)

| Capability | Import or Config | What It Unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keyframed 3-point lighting, fog, practicals, IBL, godrays | `film-stage/lights`, `film-stage/lights-meta`, `src/timeline/lights.json` | `createLightingRig`, `applyLightingRig`, `lightsAt`, `LIGHT_PRESETS`, `KELVIN_CHIPS`, `createEnvironment` (`ENVIRONMENT_PRESETS`: `studio` / `outdoor` / `night` IBL), `createGodraysPass` (`three-good-godrays`, `PointLight`/`DirectionalLight` only, before `ToneMappingEffect`), `practicalIntensityAt` |
| Practical lamp flicker & whole-frame fades | `film-stage/flicker`, `film-stage/fade` | `flickerAt` (deterministic lamp/screen flicker), `blackAt`, `makeFade` |
| Emissive noun highlight pulses | `film-stage/highlight` | `createHighlight` + `pulseEnvelope` for synchronized ON/OFF emissive glow accents on key props/parts without mutating shared materials |
| 3D cutaway / cross-section slice planes | `renderer.localClippingEnabled = true` (`src/scene.ts`) | Attach `THREE.Plane` to `material.clippingPlanes` (or fade alpha walls) for architectural cutaways and X-ray reveals |
| Depth of field & timed rack focus | `film-stage/dof`, `shots.json` `optics.focus`, `cam.json` `focus` | `makeDof`, `blurPx` with meter distance, named 3D target (`Steve.head`), or world point; final aperture sampling via `tools.apertureSampling` |

| Camera rails, speed matching, ortho & shake | `film-stage/camera`, `film-stage/camera-meta`, `src/timeline/cam.json`, `src/timeline/shots.json` | `cameraAt`, `makeCameraRail`, `cameraRailU`, `cameraMotionSpans`, `defaultCameraHandles`, `resolveCameraPoint`, `canMatchCameraSpeed`, `orthographicCamera`, `fitBoxFrame`, `applyCameraRig`, `applyCameraFeel`, `orientCamera`, `projectCamera`, `inView`, `onScreenRanges`, `resolveFocusDistance`, `focalLengthAt`, `opticalFocusAt`, `shakeAt`, `EASES`, per-channel `easeChannels`, `{ target, offset, space }` anchors, and per-shot `cameraClock: "output"` on `shots.json` |
| Shot animations & WebGL shader transitions | `film-stage/animate`, `film-stage/animate-meta`, `film-stage/transitions`, `film-stage/transitions-meta`, `film-stage/transition-extension` | `animate`, `ANIMS` (whips, pushes, hits), `makeTransitions`, `TRANSITIONS` (38 built-in GLSL shaders including `dissolve`, `cardFlip`, `pushUp`, `irisOpen`, `whipStreak`, `glitchBreak`), plus custom GLSL via `makeTransitions(..., extra)` or `registerTransitionExtension` |

| Kinetic typography, shapes & 15 bundled fonts | `film-stage/titles`, `film-stage/titles-catalog`, `film-stage/titles-text`, `src/timeline/text.json` | `makeTitles`, `FONTS` (15 bundled fonts: 14 variable `@fontsource-variable/*` fonts including Inter, Geist, Bricolage Grotesque, DM Sans + static IBM Plex Mono), `typed`, `scrambled`, `counted`, callout circles/arrows |
| HTML/CSS/SVG/Canvas/WebGL overlay compositing | `film-stage/openfilm`, `film-stage/openfilm-page`, `film-stage/openfilm-recipes`, `src/timeline/openfilm.json` | `src/timeline/openfilm.json` is auto-composited by Studio & headless render via `vite_film.mjs`; programmatic `makeOpenFilmLayers`, `loadOpenFilmPage` composite any deterministic HTML/SVG/Canvas/WebGL page exposing `window.film.frame(t)` |
| Live Studio film runtime, labs & scene hooks | `live-studio/edit`, `live-studio/lips`, `live-studio/lab`, `live-studio/targets` | `EDIT`, `setEdit`, `toSource`, `fromSource`; `mouthCues`; `post`, `log`, `config`, `canvas`, `still`, `done` (`lab/*.ts` harnesses); `findTarget`, `targetHelp`, `labelOf`, `inside`, `hue`, `label`, `chart`, `mark`; plus `draw.checks`, `draw.describe`, `draw.renderFrame`, `draw.tools` hooks |

## 2. Procedural Acting, Motion, and Camera Math (`packages/film-kit` — All 38 Modules)

Every module below imports directly as `film-kit/<name>` and computes purely from time `t` (zero hidden state, 100% scrub-safe):

| Category | Modules (`film-kit/<name>`) | Core Capabilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Life & Micro-Acting | `idle`, `blinks`, `drowsy`, `yawn`, `sigh`, `gaze`, `attention`, `express`, `micro`, `tremor` | Breathing/sway/gasp/settle, natural lid blinks & drowsy blinks, yawns, sighs, eye-leads-head pursuit & fixations, attention chains, facial mood shifts, micro-noise, hand/arm tremor |
| Reactive & Physical Acting | `startle`, `freeze`, `hesitate`, `overlap`, `cartoon`, `effort`, `notice`, `touch`, `steady`, `reach`, `handeye`, `balance`, `posture`, `lean`, `steer`, `explore` | Jolt/startle reactions, freeze holds, hesitation stretches, secondary overlap lag, `cartoon` (SIGGRAPH 2006 FK angular anticipation & overshoot filter), effort phrasing, notice pulls, `touches` (spontaneous self-face touches / adaptors), `steady` (actor head stabilization / vestibulocollic reflex over moving trunk), minimum-jerk/Fitts reaches, hand-eye coordination, balance/posture/lean/steering/exploration |
| Curves, Springs & Poses | `curve`, `spring`, `arc`, `transition`, `pose`, `hash` | `pchip`/`hermite` splines, damped spring tracks & target following (`springTrack`, `followTarget`), `arc` (spherical shoulder-pivoted limb arcs), Bollo inertialization & `quintic` / `chain` transitions, pose blending/unwrapping (`throughPose`, `addPose`, `scalePose`), deterministic seeded hashing |
| Minecraft Physics & Footsteps | `mcmotion`, `steps` | Authentic Minecraft movement kinematics (`mcBody`, `jumpArc`, 20 TPS friction) and footstep contacts mapped to block surface sounds (`contacts`, `gameSteps`, `stepSounds`) |
| Camera Optics & Safe Framing | `lens`, `handheld`, `shake`, `safe` | `PRIME_LENSES`, `fovOf`/`mmOf`, `dollyZoom`, `frameSubject`, `distanceToFill`, `dofParams`, `SHOT_SIZES`, `handheld` (recorded CC0 Camera Shakify wobble presets `SHAKES`), impact `shake`, and 16:9 EBU R 95 `safe` margins (9:16 Shorts UI chrome clearance is enforced by `--check text`) |
